# The Laboratory of Dr. Varnholt

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** Castle Varnholt, above the village of Grausbach, on Halloween night in a great storm.
- **The threat:** Dr. Emeric Varnholt, a grieving surgeon, and his Bride.
- **The prize:** the Galvanic Heart, the core of the coil that powers the Bride.

## The grief
Ten years ago fever took Elsa, wife of the surgeon **Dr. Emeric Varnholt**. He took her body to his castle and has robbed the village graveyard since, learning to restart dead flesh with lightning. When he could no longer remember her face, he burned her portrait (room 8).

## The castle
His creations guard the castle: **Stitched Hounds**, **Patchwork Horrors**, **Galvanic Servants** and **Brain in a Jar** constructs.

## The Bride
Tonight his storm has come. In the tower laboratory (room 12, above the generator in room 9) he means to raise Elsa in a stitched body: **the Bride**. The villagers want their dead back and the storm stopped.

## Running the castle
- **The round gate.** Between rooms 3 and 5. The **Bird-Shaped Key** is in the hand of a dead village knight (room 11); otherwise pick it (DC 18 thieves' tools) or force it (DC 20 Athletics).
- **The story door.** Touching the knob of the door into room 14 shows Elsa's death.
- **The doctor.** Epic commander of his Servants. At 0 Hit Points while the Bride lives, he drops to 1 instead and flees up to her, once.
- **The coil.** While it runs, lightning arcs between its coils: anyone in the arcs is shocked (DC 19 Con, 2d8 + 9 lightning), and the Bride regains 20 HP there. Wreck it (AC 18, 60 HP, or an action and a DC 20 Strength or Intelligence check) to kill the arcs and strip her Epic Boss Resistance; the GM switch outside the room's east wall turns the arcs off.
- **Light.** Dark except rooms 5 (jars), 9 (generator), 12 (coil) and 13 (lamp).

## Details invented
- Coil: the arcs heal the Bride 20 HP when she enters or starts her turn in them; checks are Athletics or Arcana.
- Pulling the Heart (an action, after the coil or Bride falls) breaks the storm by dawn.
- Traps: Floor, find DC 15, cut wires DC 15; Portrait, find DC 15, douse it; Gas, find DC 16 Investigation, Poisoned 1 hour, close the pump valve; Cage, find DC 16, escape DC 16, wedge the catch DC 16.
- Knight: Sir Benedikt Kohl, killed by the gas; the key lay in the first lord's coffin; the crest is a raven.
- Room 4's feast is laid each wedding anniversary; room 14 is cleaned daily, its portrait's face rubbed away, its door bar free.
- Brains: schoolmaster and priest (6), village doctor (13). Ledger, labels and tags name the dead.
- The Bride's veil is Elsa's wedding lace; the locket holds her hair.
- Room 15 held his grave diggers.
- Treasure spots: desk (2), tureen (4), drawer (6), coat (7), portraits (8), cart (10), tray and lamp (13), boxes (14), the Bride's neck and under the table (12).
