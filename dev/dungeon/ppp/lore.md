# The Puppeteer's Playhouse

## Read-aloud
Rain runs off the shuttered front of the Gilded Thimble Playhouse. Its doors closed a year ago, after Ambrose Quill's last show, and no one has bought a ticket since. Yet people still go missing on this street. Three nights ago Lord Edric Ashgrove went inside to look for his sister Mirabel, who sang in that last show. He has not come out, and his household has asked you to bring him home. Above the doors a painted sign still shows a smiling puppet on its strings. Behind them, faint and patient, a music box is playing.

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** the Gilded Thimble Playhouse, a shuttered city puppet theatre and toymaker's house.
- **The threat:** Mistress Tangle, the Spinner Above, a fey spider-thing who strings puppets with stolen souls.
- **The prize:** the Soul Spool in the fly loft (room 12); cutting it free frees every soul in the house.

## The bargain
Ambrose Quill, puppet master of the Gilded Thimble, wanted puppets that dance without strings. He bargained with Mistress Tangle, who lived above his stage. She gave his puppets life by stringing them with soul-thread spun from people: grown townsfolk who came to the shows and never went home.

## The mouthpiece
When Quill tried to stop her, Tangle made him her ventriloquist's mouthpiece instead. He sits dead in his dressing-room armchair (room 8), and his three dummies still talk in his voice.

## The house
Shut for a year, the playhouse still swallows people. **Tinwhisker Swarms**, clockwork mice Quill built for a children's matinee, nest in the auditorium seats. **Marionette Dancers** hang from the ceilings on Tangle's threads. **Porcelain Dolls** hold stolen souls and move only when no one is looking at them. **Ventriloquist's Dummies** crave an audience. **The Prima Marionette**, Tangle's favourite, keeps a living man as her dance partner.

## The noble
Lord Edric Ashgrove, patron of the theatre, came looking for his missing sister, Mirabel. He is bound to a chair in the workshop (room 10) by puppet strings and is turning to wood from the hands up. Mirabel's soul is in a Porcelain Doll in the Doll Parlour (room 6). Her doll wears a scarf matching the one in the Costume Wardrobe strongbox (room 5).

## Running the playhouse
- **Talking.** Every thinking creature can be bargained with; each room journal says how. Dolls and swarms cannot, but a doll goes still while watched.
- **The fire curtain.** The iron fire curtain between the Stage Wings (11) and the Fly Loft (12) is locked. The **Stage Manager's Key** in the Scenery Dock (14) opens it; otherwise pick it (DC 18 thieves' tools) or force it (DC 20 Athletics).
- **The pin rail.** Tangle's strings are tied off to four ropes on the pin rail along the west wall of the fly loft (each AC 15, 25 HP, or an action and a DC 16 Athletics or Sleight of Hand check to untie). While one rope stands, Tangle has Epic Boss Resistance. When all four are cut she falls, takes 4d10 bludgeoning damage and is Prone.
- **The Spool.** Once Tangle is dead, cutting the Spool free (an action) frees every soul: the dolls and puppets go still, the townsfolk wake on the stage floor, and Lord Ashgrove's hands turn back to flesh.
- **The fortune-teller.** The dwarf automaton (13) deals each character one card, once.
- **Light.** Dark except rooms 1 (ticket booth lamp), 10 (workbench lamp), 12 (threads) and 13 (crystal ball).
