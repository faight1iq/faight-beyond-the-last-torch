# Basilica of the Thunder Crown

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** a ruined basilica of the storm god Vauroth, lost for years and now overrun by rabbits.
- **The threat:** lightning-touched hares in every hall, bronze guardians that still patrol, and the Thunderbuck in the sanctum.
- **The prize:** the Thunder Crown, Vauroth's relic, which rests on the high altar and still leaks lightning.

## The basilica and the crown
The basilica was the house of Vauroth, god of storms. Its priests kept his relic, the **Thunder Crown**, on the high altar of the sanctum, and lightning answered their prayers. They built bronze guardians, the **Stormguard Sentinels**, and warded the inner halls with storm runes and alarms.

The priests are long gone, and for years the basilica was considered lost. The crown never stopped leaking lightning.

## The rabbits
Rabbits nested in the empty basilica. They do not care about Vauroth or his house, but over the decades the ones that grew up near the crown changed. Some became **Warren Swarms**, masses of crackling feral rabbits. Others grew into **Thunder Hares**, as large as horses, with static in their fur. The ones that nest closest to the sanctum are **Galvanic Jackalopes**, antlered hares whose copper-green antlers arc lightning. Their alpha is **the Thunderbuck**, a huge hare-stag that nests on the high altar. Its antlers have grown around the Thunder Crown.

## The sentinels
The Stormguard Sentinels still follow their orders. They attack anyone who is not a priest of Vauroth, and that includes the hares. Whoever holds the Thunder Crown can speak with the sentinels and command them to stand down.

## The goblins
A band of goblin scavengers came for the basilica's treasure. Most died. The last, Snikkit, lies dying in the goblins' camp. If the party heals him, he warns them about the Thunderbuck and tells them about the hidden door into the sanctum, then asks to leave.

## Running the basilica
- **The front gate is locked.** The battered gate between the narthex (room 1) and the treasury (room 3) needs the Iron Key, thieves' tools, or force.
- **The key is the path.** The open route runs through the warren nave (room 2) and the chapel (room 5) to the reliquary (room 14), where sentinels guard the **Iron Key** under the debris.
- **The vestry door wails.** Opening it in room 7 sets off a thunder trap and calls the jackalopes from room 6.
- **There are secret routes.** A secret door leads from the narthex into the choir passage (room 4), and another leads from the choir passage straight into the sanctum (room 9), bypassing the storm glyph in room 8. The vestry ledger names Vauroth, and speaking his name disarms that glyph.
- **The Thunderbuck is the end.** It fights on the altar dais with two jackalopes. When it falls, the crown can be worked free of its antlers.
