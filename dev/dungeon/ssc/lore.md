# Scarletskull Castle

## Read-aloud
A notice nailed up in every inn along the border road offers a reward from Clan Ironvein for word of their kinsman Brannoc, who set out a season ago for Scarletskull Castle and the sealed vault said to lie inside it. He has not come back. On the old road to the castle you have passed grey stone figures: a carter with his whip raised, a pedlar caught mid-stride, a mule still in its harness. They were travellers once. Now you stand at the castle's ruined gate, its red-grey walls slumped and scorched by a battle fought a thousand years ago, its great doors hanging open on the dark. Somewhere inside, something scratches and clucks.

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** Scarletskull Castle, destroyed in a great battle a millennium ago and uninhabited since. Recently a pack of basilisks has made its lair here.
- **The threat:** **The Basilisk Matriarch** in the Scarlet Vault (room 3), and her pack throughout the castle.
- **The prize:** the vault's gold and the **Gauntlets of Ogre Power** (room 3), and the **Giant Slayer Battleaxe** in the Collapsed Armoury (room 13).

## The fall of Scarletskull
The castle was the seat of Lord Aldous Varr, the Scarlet Lord, whose banner was a red skull. A thousand years ago the warlord Ulgrath the Stonemaker besieged it, driving before his army a vast basilisk called the Grey Mother. Varr's court mage, Magister Ilsabet Corr, made the **Sunstone** (room 10) against her gaze, and the defenders killed the beast in the vault with four bronze mirrors, but the castle fell anyway. The matriarch now laired in the vault is the Grey Mother's descendant.

## The dwarf
Brannoc Ironvein came for the vault a month ago. He found the armourer's brass key, was struck down by the glare of the **Mirrorscale Basilisks** in the Hall of Mirrors (room 9), and crawled to the storeroom of room 14 to die. His journal names where the other two keys lie.

## Running the castle
- **The vault door.** The double door from the Rear Postern (room 5) into the Scarlet Vault (room 3) has three keyholes. The three **Brass Keys of Scarletskull** open it: the Strongroom key (room 11), the Dwarf's key (room 14) and the Coffer key (room 15).
- **The secret door.** The passage north from the Hall of the Petrified Host (room 2) ends at a secret door into the vault: DC 18 Wisdom (Perception), or the Sunstone's light shows its outline.
- **The postern.** Room 5's north double door is a second way in, barred from inside.
- **The mirrors.** See room 3.
- **Statues.** Every statue is a petrified victim of some age; Greater Restoration, or the gullet fluid of a slain basilisk, restores one.
- **Light.** Dark except rooms 2 and 13 (daylight through the broken roof), 3 (green glow from the nest) and 10 (the Sunstone).
