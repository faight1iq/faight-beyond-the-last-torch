# The Ironmark Stockade

## Read-aloud
The bells rang the peace three days ago, but no bells ring at Ironmark. Gharrek the Unbowed's war banner still flies over the gate, and the drawbridge stays raised against the world. Behind those walls, forty soldiers of the Crown wait in chains for a rescue the treaty says cannot come. A new gallows stands in the courtyard, built since the war ended. At every dawn, Gharrek hangs one more. The sun is going down. You have until first light.

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** the Ironmark Stockade, a hobgoblin frontier prison fortress.
- **The threat:** Warlord Gharrek the Unbowed and his legion, who refuse the peace.
- **The prize:** about forty Crown prisoners in the basement cell blocks (B4, B5), freed before dawn.

## The holdout
The Crown and the legions signed the peace three days ago at the eleventh bell. Gharrek refused, pulled his legion into Ironmark and shut the gates. He hangs one prisoner from the courtyard gallows (G4) at every dawn until the Crown resumes the war. The Crown cannot send its army without breaking the peace, so it sends a strike team. The Iron Captain is his lieutenant.

Every creature is awake, armed and visible; there is no ambush. Servants run to raise the alarm; the GM may add Doom when any creature escapes.

## The fortress
- **Ground floor (G):** moat, gatehouse, courtyard, outbuildings, four corner towers and the keep.
- **Basement (B):** culvert, sluice, guard room, cell blocks, interrogation, warden's office. Stairs from the Kitchen (G14) to the Cellar Stores (B8).
- **Upper floor (U, W):** tower tops joined by wall-walks, the Winch Room (W1) and Gharrek's floor. Stairs from the Grand Stair (G16) to the Upper Gallery (U1), and from each corner tower (G8 to G11) to its top (W6 to W9). The throne room balcony bridges to the north wall-walk.
- **Secret culvert:** from the west moat wall (G2) to the Moat Culvert (B1).

## Getting in
- **The drawbridge** is raised. The windlass in the Winch Room (W1) lowers it.
- **The moat** is deep water; creatures must swim.
- **The culvert.** A swimmer who searches the west moat wall finds the grate with a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check. It is the shortest escape route.
- **The walls.** The tower stairs lead up to the wall-walks.

## The prisoners
About twenty Crown soldiers are held in each cell block (B4, B5). Warden Skarn (B6) carries the Warden's Master Keys, which open every cell. Without them, each cell door needs a DC 18 thieves' tools check or a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check. Freed prisoners barricade their cell block and wait; they do not follow or fight. The Forgotten Roll in the War Room (U2) lists every prisoner and the hanging order.

## Ending
The adventure ends when Gharrek dies, or when the fourth Legion Standard in the Throne Room (U5) falls and he is broken. Either way, the legion surrenders.

## Creatures
- **Ironmark Servant:** unarmed, 1 HP.
- **Ironmark Legionnaire, Iron Guard:** soldiers.
- **Legion Squad, Legion Platoon, Legion Company:** 5, 10 and 20 soldiers, one swarm token each.
- **War Hound Pack:** one swarm token; **Kennelmaster Vosk** leads it.
- **Moat Eel, Leech Swarm:** in the moat.
- **Old Gutter:** river troll chained in the sluice.
- **Forgemaster Ulgra Coalhand, Sergeant-at-Arms Drekk, Warden Skarn:** officers.
- **The Iron Captain:** lieutenant.
- **Gharrek the Unbowed:** boss; Sword of Wounding.
