## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** the sand-choked halls of an astronomer-mage, long abandoned.
- **The threat:** scorpions everywhere, ogres in the north wing, and the Void Matriarch in the inner halls.
- **The choice:** the Void Shard is a prize, but it feeds the Matriarch. Taking it or destroying it ends her power to grow.

## The Savant and the shard
The Void Savant was an astronomer-mage who studied the empty dark between the stars. In his last years he drew something out of that dark: the **Void Shard**, a sliver of nothing that is cold to the touch.

The Savant could not destroy it. He locked it in a mechanical safe in a columned vault and sealed the vault behind a round ironclad double door. The door opens only with the **Iron Key**. He hid the key among his stores and retired to his sanctum, where he died in his reading chair. A crack of black void glass opened in the floor behind him and never closed. He is long dead, and people still keep away from the halls.

## The scorpions
Desert scorpions found the halls through the cracked walls and sand drifts. They do not care about the Savant or his work. Most are ordinary giant **Dune Scorpions** and swarms of smaller ones. But void energy leaks from the shard and from the rift in the sanctum, and the scorpions that nest closest to it have changed. Their shells have turned black and glassy, and they move in ways that are hard to follow. The largest of them is the **Void Matriarch**, who nests in the ritual hall beside the sanctum and grows stronger the longer the shard stays in the halls.

## The ogres
A band of ogres moved into the north wing and made a den in the largest hall. They hunt scorpions for food and loot whatever they can carry. They tracked a tribal shaman into the far north chamber and killed him there; his remains still hold a handful of gold.

## Running the halls
- **Sand everywhere.** Footing is poor near the drifts. A sellsword lies dead off the Star Rotunda (room 9).
- **The scorpions escalate.** Dune Scorpions near the entrance, swarms in the brood halls, void-touched scorpions deeper in, then the Matriarch.
- **The ogres are a choice.** The north wing is optional. The ogres can be fought, avoided, or bargained with.
- **The Iron Key is the path.** The key is in the rotten chest in the storeroom (room 14). It opens the round door (room 16) to the vault, the void-scarred hall, and the Matriarch.
- **The hearth is a way out.** The cold violet fire in room 4 sends anyone who touches it outside the halls, to the entrance corridor.
- **The shard is the turning point.** If the party takes the Void Shard from the safe, the Matriarch knows. She comes for it.
