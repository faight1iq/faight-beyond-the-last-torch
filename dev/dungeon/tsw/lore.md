# Tomb of the Storm Witch

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** the flooded tomb of a witch who called storms over the marsh.
- **The threat:** the drowned knights who killed her, the wraiths of her coven, and an undead venomous worm and its brood.
- **The prize:** Soride, a legendary lantern.

## The witch and the knights
A company of knights hunted down the Storm Witch. With her dying curse she flooded the tomb and drowned them. They rose as **Drowned Knights**, bound to guard her tomb against everyone. Her coven sisters were bound into the Hall of the Coven as **Tempest Wraiths**.

Since then the tomb has changed hands many times: bandits, cultists, and a mad painter who painted the swamps on the gallery wall (room 13).

## The worm
Recently **the Corpse Worm** burrowed up into the witch's burial chamber (room 9). It swallowed **Soride, the Storm Lantern**, and the lantern's pale green-white light glows through its rotting hide. Its larvae, the **Venom Grubs**, hatch from leathery egg sacs. Creatures its venom kills rise as **Wormridden Corpses**, with grubs nesting inside them.

## Running the tomb
- **The Mouth.** Room 3's west wall is a locked double door carved as a huge mouth. The **Silver Tongue Key** in the Hidden Vestry (room 2) opens it safely. Picking or forcing it triggers the Biting Mouth.
- **The long ways.** Without the key, the party can still reach room 5 by the east stairs of room 3, or by its south door and a secret door.
- **The secret doors.** Secret doors hide room 2 and join the Witch's Study (room 12) to the Lair (room 9).
- **The crossroads.** North of the Trunk Room (room 10), stairs lead to rooms 9, 12 and 13, and the West Passage runs back to rooms 4, 2 and 1.
- **Grounding the crevice.** The copper lightning rod in the Hall of the Coven (room 6) can ground the Storm Crevice in room 8.
- **The boss.** The Corpse Worm fights with two Wormridden Corpses. Soride is cut from its body.

## Details invented
- All secret doors: DC 16 Wisdom (Perception).
- The Mouth: pick DC 16, force DC 18 Strength (both trigger it); find DC 15 Investigation; wedge the jaws DC 16 Athletics.
- Room 2 coffer: DC 15 thieves' tools or DC 15 Strength.
- Storm Crevice: find DC 15 Arcana; ground it with the room 6 rod (DC 13 Strength to pull free) or *dispel magic* DC 15.
- Hexed Trunk: a cultist's hex; find DC 16 Perception; *dispel magic* DC 16.
- Storm Glyph Ring: find DC 15 Arcana; *dispel magic* DC 16.
- Room 7 water is 2 feet deep (difficult terrain).
- Treasure locations: robber's pack (1), niche (5), boat (7), knights' chest (11), dead cultist (14).
- Room 11's two empty biers belong to room 12's knights.
