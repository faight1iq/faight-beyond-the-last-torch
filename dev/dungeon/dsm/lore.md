# Darkspear Mausoleum

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** the tomb of a dwarf lord, deep in the jungle, now overrun with bears.
- **The threat:** bears in every hall, oozes that dissolve the dead, undead dwarf guards that still defend the tomb, and the Elder Ursa in the great tomb.
- **The prize:** the Thane's sealed treasury, and the Darkspear itself.

## The tomb and the Thane
Thane Brokk Darkspear led a dwarf expedition into the jungle, and he died there. His clan did not carry him home. They built his tomb where he fell, laid him in a great stone sarcophagus with his black iron spear, the **Darkspear**, and sealed his treasury behind a massive double door that needs two golden keys turned together. They left an honor guard behind.

Then the jungle swallowed the mausoleum, and the clan never came back.

## The bears
Bears moved into the empty tomb. The **Moss Bears** are huge jungle bears with moss growing in their fur. Deeper in, a pale fungus grows from the rubble and the old dead, and the bears that eat it or sleep in it become **Spore-Mad Bears**, driven to rage. Human tomb robbers came before the party; they scratched DWARFS SUCK on the shrine wall, and their bones lie in the bear den. The matriarch of the bears is **the Elder Ursa**, a colossal bear that sleeps in the great tomb itself. The Darkspear is still lodged in her shoulder from the night she killed the last dwarf guardian.

## The oozes
The **Tomb Jellies** grew from the embalming vats. They dissolve anything left lying. One digested a dwarf adventurer in the crypt.

## The wardens
The **Darkspear Wardens** are the Thane's dwarf honor guard, risen as armored undead. They defend the treasury and the tomb, and they hate the bears. Returning the Darkspear to the Thane's sarcophagus lays them to rest.

## Running the mausoleum
- **The two keys route.** The treasury door in the Hall of Two Keys (room 10) cannot be picked and can be forced only with a DC 25 Strength check. One Golden Key is in the box at the pool in room 8, guarded by strangling vines. The other hovers in a warded ring in room 15, reached through the spear trap in room 13. Speaking the Thane's name (carved in room 1) in Dwarvish disarms that ward.
- **The secret routes.** A secret door joins the Spear Gallery (room 13) to the treasury (room 12), so the party can enter the treasury without both keys. Another secret door in room 10 opens onto the corridor to the great tomb (room 14). Both need a DC 18 Wisdom (Perception) check.
- **The boss.** The Elder Ursa fights in the great tomb with two Spore-Mad Bears. When she falls, the Darkspear can be pulled from her shoulder and returned to the sarcophagus.
