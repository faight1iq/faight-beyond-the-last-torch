# Mystery Sepulcher of the Fallen King

## Read-aloud
Far out past the last fishing grounds lies Skerrow, a storm-beaten island empty for a hundred years. Sailors say the sepulcher of its Fallen King is still out there, and that untold treasures of magical artifacts lie in it. A month ago a hobgoblin warband sailed for Skerrow to take them, and none of the hobgoblins has come back. Now your own boat grinds onto the shingle of a grey cove. Above you, a stair cut into the sea cliff climbs to a pair of tall stone doors. One of them stands open.

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** the sepulcher of the Fallen King on the island of Skerrow, half drowned by the sea.
- **The threat:** the Fallen King, undead in his tomb (room 15), and Warlord Vrask Ironjaw's hobgoblin warband.
- **The prize:** the King's grave goods and the **Mace of Disruption** in his sarcophagus (room 15).

## The broken oath
King Hadrek of Skerrow swore to his honour guard, the Fallen Legion, that he would live or die beside them. When raiders came, he barred the sepulcher's sea-gate behind himself and his court and left the Legion to die on the beach. The Legion's dead came back up out of the sea and drowned him in the pool of the Hall of the Kneeling Knights (room 8). Their oath still binds them as **Barrow Wights**, and it binds him: four bronze urns around his sarcophagus hold his drowned oath. The songs say the Legion betrayed him; the fresco in room 13 shows the truth, guarded by his courtiers, now **Court Shades**.

## The tide-monks
A small order of tide-monks, the Brothers of the Ebb, kept the tomb. One is left: Brother Tamsin, dying in the Monk's Grotto (room 9) while three **Hobgoblin Devastators** torture him for the words that open the bronze door. He knows the King's true name and the urns' weakness.

## The warband
**Warlord Vrask Ironjaw** camps in the Hall of the Fallen Legion (room 5) and holds the Flooded Antechamber (room 2) with **Hobgoblin Shieldbearers**. **Gravecrawler Swarms**, carrion crabs from the sea caves, fill the Drowned Chapel (room 4).

## Running the sepulcher
- **The bronze door.** The huge door between rooms 5 and 8 is locked. It opens to the King's true name, Morvane, spoken with a hand on the bronze. Brother Tamsin and the fresco (room 13) both give it. Otherwise pick it (DC 20 thieves' tools) or force it (DC 22 Strength (Athletics)). Vrask has no key.
- **The urns.** See room 15. Holy water (room 11) destroys one outright.
- **The signet.** The **Fallen King's Signet** (room 7) makes the Barrow Wights hesitate to attack its wearer.
- **The stairs down** in room 15 lead nowhere: flooded and sealed by rubble.
- **Light.** Dark except rooms 5 (campfire), 9 (brazier), 13 (the fresco, while looked at) and 15 (blue witch-light).
