# Archive of the Leper Oracle

## At a glance
- **Party:** one level 10 character, solo.
- **The place:** deep in the dark wood, in uncharted lands.
- **The threat:** Mother Ysolde, the Leper Oracle; and the Bloodtusk orcs who squatted the archive.
- **The prize:** countless treasures of magical artifacts and ancient books.

## The Oracle
Mother Ysolde was a seer of the dark wood. Each vision cost her flesh to the **Grey Rot**, a magical blight. Her scribes copied every prophecy here. She died on her seat (room 12); the rot keeps her undead, still whispering. Her dead **Rot Scribes** still copy in room 3.

## The intruders
The treasure hunter Corvin Ashmere came first; the Crystal Ward turned him to violet crystal (room 5). Then the **Bloodtusk** orcs under **Gruk Halfear** broke through the side door from room 4 into the Pedestal Hall (9).

## Running the archive
- **Two routes in.** Through the orc camp (4, 9, 10), or through the stone door in room 8 with both crown keys. Both meet at room 10.
- **The keys.** The mosaic (2) shows a waxing and a waning crown. The **Key of the Waxing Crown** lies beside Corvin (5); the **Key of the Waning Crown** lies under the rubble in room 7 (the Rubble Warden).
- **The flask.** Gruk claims the Potion of Invisibility. If it is still there, he drinks it when Bloodied.
- **The sealed door.** The 8-11 door is barred on room 11's side: lifted there with no check; from 8, DC 22 Strength.
- **The Oracle's weakness.** Four piles of her prophecy scrolls ring the dais. Burning a pile (an action with fire, or a DC 15 Strength check to tip it into a brazier) makes her scream. When two piles have burned, she loses her Epic Boss Resistance.

## Details invented (review)
- Names: Mother Ysolde, Gruk Halfear, Corvin Ashmere, Snag (dead orc, room 1).
- Rot Needle: find DC 15 Investigation; disarm DC 15 thieves' tools, Mage Hand; Poisoned 1 hour.
- Crystal Ward: within 5 feet of Corvin or the key; find DC 15 Arcana or Perception; suppress DC 15 Arcana or Dispel Magic; Restrained, escape DC 15 Athletics.
- Rot Mist: middle of the gallery; find DC 16 Perception; burning a side's veils spends it; Advantage holding breath; resets after 10 minutes.
- Mosaic: DC 12 History. Room 10: 10 minutes reading, one true answer.
- Orcs fight from half cover; a room 2 raider flees to warn Gruk. Gruk drinks as a Bonus Action.
- Stone door (8-10): both keys turned within a round; cannot be picked or forced; opens freely from 10.
- Each burned pile also costs the Oracle her next Response; four piles.
- Light: dark except rooms 3 (candles), 9 (fire), 12 (braziers).
- Treasure spots: Snag's belt (1), satchel (3), basket (6), loot sack (9), reliquary niche (10), her body and lap (12).
