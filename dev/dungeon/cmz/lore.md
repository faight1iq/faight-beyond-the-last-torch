# The Hollow King's Maze

## At a glance
- **Party:** level 10, 6 characters.
- **The place:** a corn maze that grew overnight over the Hollowmere Harvest Festival, at night.
- **The threat:** Mother Gristle, the Harvest Witch, and the headless Hollow King she made.
- **The prize:** the Harvest Ember, the festival's stolen hearth-fire.

## The bargain
After years of failed harvests, Farmer Jack Hollow bargained with a hag, **Mother Gristle**, for an endless harvest. Her price was his head, taken on festival night. He rose headless as **the Hollow King**, riding a black nightmare horse, with a carved jack-o'-lantern for a head.

## The maze
That night the corn grew into a maze and swallowed the festival-goers. Scarecrows walk as **Scarecrow Stalkers**, crows serve them as **Murder Crow Swarms**, the husks knot into **Husk Stranglers**, and carved pumpkins wake as **Lantern Gourdlings**. **The Harvest Witch** tends the maze from her cauldron (room 10).

## The Harvest Ember
The witch stole the festival's hearth-fire and set it in the King's pumpkin head. He waits at the heart of the maze (room 13).

## Running the maze
- **The locked gate.** Room 13 is reached only through the locked gate in the east side of room 10. The **Iron Harvest Key** hangs in the Scarecrow Shrine (room 14); otherwise pick it (DC 18 thieves' tools) or force it (DC 20 Athletics).
- **The witch.** While she lives, Scarecrow Stalkers that drop to 0 Hit Points in her clearing rise again at her next turn with half their Hit Points, once each.
- **Plucking the Ember.** An action within 5 feet of the King and a DC 20 Athletics or Sleight of Hand check pulls out the Ember: 4d10 radiant damage, and he loses his Epic Boss Resistance. Killing him frees it too.
- **Light.** Dark except rooms 1 and 6 (orange), 10 (green) and 13 (the King's fire).

## Details invented
- Spot a still Scarecrow Stalker: DC 18 Perception within 10 feet.
- Rotten Gourd Patch: find DC 15 Perception; Poisoned until a Short Rest; walk the edge or burn the gourds.
- The Thresher: find DC 16 Investigation; jam it with DC 16 Athletics or crawl behind it.
- Grave Hands: find DC 15 Perception; escape DC 15 Athletics; holy water or reach from 10 feet.
- Bobbing Barrel: find DC 16 Perception; Frightened until the end of the next turn; tip it over from 10 feet or *dispel magic* DC 16.
- Room 7's barred gate lifts without a check.
- Room 9 is the grave of Amos Hollow, Jack's father; the rapier is his.
- Room 8's body is a hired sellsword who burned the wagon.
- Room 7's necklace was the Harvest Queen's; room 11's barrel holds a drowned festival-goer.
- Treasure spots: ticket booth (1), festival-goer's pouch (2), prize chest (5), witch's belt (10), pumpkin pile (12); the King wields the Flame Tongue.
- Room 15: caved-in potato store; Jack buried the strongbox.
